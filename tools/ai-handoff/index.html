Which job should I hand to AI?

List the jobs that eat your week. This ranks which one to hand off first, and how many hours you might get back.

Add 3 to 10 tasks you actually do. For each one, put the hours and move three sliders. The score is on this page. Nothing is sent to a server, and there is no login.

The math, in the open

Each slider is 1 to 5. Add the three numbers, divide by 15, and multiply by 100. That is the score. A 5, 5, and 5 scores 100. A 1, 1, and 1 scores 20.

Score = (Repetitive + Rule-based + Text/data) ÷ 15 × 100

Hours you might get back are a conservative percent of the weekly hours. A perfect score still only counts 55%. The rest stays with you for review, exceptions, and the week that does not go to plan.

Tasks are ranked by score. If two scores match, the one that gives back more hours comes first. The top task is the cleanest handoff, which is not always the longest one.

The middle slider also picks the kind of help:

  • 4 or 5, mostly rules: transport. You are moving information and the meaning stays the same. A script fits.
  • 1 or 2, mostly judgment: translation. Someone has to read it and decide what it means. An AI agent fits. When I say an AI agent, I mean a tool like Grok. You still check what comes back.
  • 3: both. A script moves what never changes. An agent drafts what needs a judgment. You keep the exceptions.

This is a rough sort, not a quote.
